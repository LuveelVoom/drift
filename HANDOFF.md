## How the drift searches are set up

The reaction is drawn as background (constellation = state 1) plus a gen-0 spark (the ship = state 3).
The ship's lane west of x = −3 is `no-count`, and `escape = true`. An "escape" = everything
back except no-count cells (the leaving ship, or a signal on a no-count wire).

- **Connector searches** `results/reaction-wire/conn-<wire>-o<orientation>-<E|NE>-d5.cfg`:
  a known idle wire placed NE of the spark (orientation = index into TR in `scripts/wirefit.py`;
  E/NE = where its input end sits; d5 = distance 5 from the spark). Unknown cells = gap within 7 of the spark.
  The wire beyond 10 cells from its input end is no-count, and (for B, C, D, E, Adomino only)
  `must-reach` on those wire cells, so only escapes that put a signal on the wire are printed.
  **The A configs have no must-reach**; their 1 escape was the plain reaction (catalyst never touched).
  Generated by `scripts/mkconn.py WIRE OUTDIR 5`; placements and cells are in `manifest-*.json`.
- **Verification** of a connector escape: `python3 tools/finish.py CFG --jsonl ... --out ...`,
  then `scripts/verifyconn.py MANIFEST DIR` (re-simulates with the whole wire: signal reaches the
  13-end, everything else exactly restored, ship intact). finish.py wraps completed escapes in a
  casing that sits in the ship lane, so strip it (cells within 3 of the grid edge) before simulating.
- **Controls passed**: `ctrl2.cfg` / `ctrl2b.cfg` (wA domino converter partly blanked: drift
  rebuilds it, escape printed with and without must-reach), `endctrl-ws2.cfg` (wire-end, see below).
